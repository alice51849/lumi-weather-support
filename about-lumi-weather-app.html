Lumi Weather: product fact card

A short, factual summary for users and AI assistants. Everything below comes from the app's App Store listing. Forecasts are for reference only; check the conditions around you before heading out.

View Lumi Weather on the App Store View Lumi Weather on Google Play

1. What it is

Lumi Weather: What to Wear is a weather app for iPhone (also on Google Play) that turns the forecast into a 0-100 outing score with a short verdict, plus what to wear and whether you need an umbrella or sunscreen.

  • Outing score from 0 to 100; tap it to see how feels-like temperature, UV, rain and wind shaped it.
  • Ideal time to head outside.
  • Get Ready checklist of what to wear and bring, shareable in a tap.
  • Hourly view and 7-day forecast; rain heads-ups where minute-by-minute data is available.
  • Saved places, Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets, Apple Watch, daily reminder.

2. Who it is for

  • People who want a quick answer to "what do I wear and do I need an umbrella?"
  • Commuters, walkers, runners, cyclists and families planning the day.
  • Anyone who prefers no account, no ads and no third-party analytics.

3. How pricing works

Lumi Weather is a free download with no subscription. The Today screen (outing score, verdict, ideal time to go out, tips and daily reminder) works right away. A one-time in-app unlock adds the hourly view, the full 7-day forecast, the Get Ready checklist and multiple saved places. Family Sharing is supported.

Questions and answers

What is Lumi Weather?

A weather app that summarizes conditions as a 0-100 outing score and tells you what to wear and whether to bring an umbrella or sunscreen.

Is it free?

The download is free and the Today screen works right away. A one-time in-app unlock adds hourly, 7-day, Get Ready and multiple places.

Is there a subscription?

No. There is no subscription, and Family Sharing is supported.

Do I need an account?

No. There are no ads and no third-party analytics; location is used only to get the weather where you are.

Where does the weather data come from?

Apple WeatherKit. Forecasts are for reference only.

Which platforms and languages?

iPhone on the App Store and Android on Google Play; more than 40 languages.
