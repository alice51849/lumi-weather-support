Who this app is for

Lumi Weather is a general weather app for anyone who wants a quick, clear answer about whether today is a good day to go out. It works without an account and never asks for your name, date of birth, contact details, photos or health information.

Information we collect

We do not collect, store, or transmit personal data to our servers. The app has no user accounts and no sign-in. Your settings — such as your selected language, temperature unit, saved places and reminder time — and your unlock status are stored only on your device using Apple's standard on-device storage, together with the most recently loaded forecast so the app and its widgets can show it. If you use the Apple Watch app, today's forecast summary is sent directly from your iPhone to your Apple Watch. This data is removed if you delete the app.

Location

If you grant location permission, your location is used only while you are using the app, to fetch the weather for where you are. You may also add or search for places manually instead of using your current location. Your location is sent to Apple WeatherKit to retrieve the forecast, and Apple's geocoding service is used to show place names and to find the places you search for. We do not store your location on any server we control, we do not sell location data, and we do not use location for advertising. You can change or revoke location access at any time in iOS Settings.

Weather data

Lumi Weather uses Apple WeatherKit to provide weather information. Weather data is used to generate app features such as the outing score, hourly and 7-day forecasts, outfit suggestions and the best time to go out. Weather forecasts are estimates and the suggestions in the app are general guidance; actual conditions may differ.

Notifications

If you enable the optional daily reminder, the app schedules a local notification on your device at the time you choose. These notifications are generated locally and are not sent through, or tracked by, any external server.

Sharing features

The app lets you create and share an image of today's summary or your outfit checklist using the standard iOS share sheet. Sharing only happens when you choose to, and the content goes wherever you send it. We do not receive a copy.

Purchases

The optional one-time unlock is processed entirely by Apple through the App Store (StoreKit). We do not receive or store your payment details. Purchases can be restored on a new device through your Apple account.

Third parties

The app does not include third-party advertising. The app does not include third-party analytics or tracking SDKs. The only external services used are Apple's own: WeatherKit for weather data and Apple's geocoding service for place names, both governed by Apple's privacy practices.

Your choices and contact

Because no personal data leaves your device, there is no account to delete on our side. To remove all locally stored settings, simply delete the app. For any privacy question or request, contact us at hourstag.app@gmail.com and we will respond as soon as we reasonably can.

Changes to this policy

If the app's features change in a way that affects this policy, we will update this page and revise the "Last updated" date above. Continued use of the app after an update means you accept the revised policy.

Google Play (Android)

Lumi Weather is also available as a separate native Android app on Google Play (package com.alice51849.LumiWeather). The Android app has no account, no advertising, no third-party analytics and no crash-reporting or tracking SDKs, the same as iOS. Where the Android app differs:

  • Location: the Android app asks only for approximate location (Android's ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION; never precise/GPS location and never background location), and only when you tap "Use current location" — never automatically. You can use the app without it by adding or searching for places manually.
  • Weather data: forecasts come from MET Norway's Locationforecast API (CC BY 4.0) instead of Apple WeatherKit. To get a forecast, the app sends only the coordinates of the place you are viewing — your approximate location, or a place you added — rounded to 4 decimal places, over an encrypted HTTPS connection (as with any internet request, MET Norway also sees your IP address). The home screen widget refreshes the forecast for your selected place the same way. Nothing else is uploaded.
  • Place names and search: instead of Apple's geocoding service, the name of your current area and the places you search for are looked up with the Android geocoding service built into your device (on most devices provided by Google), which receives those coordinates or the search text you type.
  • Google Play Data safety: in Google Play's terms, this means the Android app "collects" approximate location and the place-search text you type, only for app functionality. This data is processed ephemerally — used only to return the forecast or place names — and is not stored on any server we control, not sold, not shared for advertising, analytics or any other purpose, and always encrypted in transit.
  • Purchases: the one-time in-app purchase is processed by Google Play Billing; we do not receive or store your payment details.
  • Deleting your data: we keep none of your data on our side, so there is nothing for us to delete. Your settings, saved places and the last loaded forecast are stored only on your device and are removed when you delete them in the app, clear the app's storage or uninstall it. For any data request, email hourstag.app@gmail.com.
  • There is no Apple Watch app, Live Activity or Lock Screen widget on Android (a home screen widget is available instead).

Everything else in this policy applies equally to the Android app.
