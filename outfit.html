What to wear today: dress by feels-like temperature, rain chance and UV

Air temperature alone rarely answers “what should I wear?”. Wind, humidity and sun change how warm it really feels. This guide lists the thresholds that Lumi Weather uses to build its Get Ready checklist, so you can use them with or without the app.

Start with the feels-like temperature

28°C and above (82°F+)Short sleeves
23–28°C (73–82°F)Short sleeves. Add a light jacket if today's lowest feels-like temperature falls below 22°C (72°F)
18–23°C (64–73°F)Light long sleeves and a light jacket
12–18°C (54–64°F)Light long sleeves, a light jacket and thin layers you can add or remove
6–12°C (43–54°F)Warm coat, thin layers and a warm hat
Below 6°C (43°F)Warm coat, warm hat, scarf and thin layers

Each range includes its lower number and stops just short of the upper one, so 23–28°C means 23°C or more but under 28°C. If it already feels like 23°C or warmer but today's lowest feels-like temperature drops below 19°C (66°F), a light jacket is added for the cooler hours.

Rain: umbrella or full rain gear

  • Highest rain chance in today's forecast is 30% or more: bring an umbrella.
  • 60% or more: raincoat, umbrella, rain boots and spare clothes.

Sun and UV: hat, sunscreen, sunglasses

The rule looks at the highest UV index of the daylight hours today.

  • UV index 3 or more: sun hat.
  • 6 or more: sun hat and sunscreen.
  • 8 or more: sun hat, sunscreen and sunglasses (the app leaves sunglasses out for the 0–2 age setting).

Heat, cold, humidity and snacks

  • Feels like 28°C (82°F) or more: water bottle. At 33°C (91°F) or more: water bottle and a mini fan.
  • Feels like 6°C (43°F) or less: gloves and a warm flask.
  • Warm and humid (22–33°C, humidity 60% or more) and not raining: bug spray.
  • Rain chance under 60% and feels like more than 8°C (46°F): a snack for the outing.

Going out with children

Lumi Weather lets you pick an age group in Settings (it is also asked during first-time setup): 0–2, 3–5 or 6–12. The outing score is stricter about extreme heat, cold and UV for the youngest group, and the checklist adds a few things:

  • 0–2: wet wipes and spare clothes every time, a stroller cover when the rain chance is 30% or more, and a blanket when it feels like 16°C (61°F) or colder.
  • 3–5: spare clothes when it feels like 26°C (79°F) or more, or the rain chance is 30% or more.
  • 6–12: the standard list above.

How Lumi Weather turns this into a checklist

Lumi Weather is an iPhone and iPad app (iOS 17 or later) that shows an outing score from 0 to 100 with a short verdict on the Today screen. The Get Ready tab applies the rules on this page to the current forecast and gives you a checklist you can tick off and share as an image. Weather data comes from Apple WeatherKit, there is no account, and the app has no ads and no third-party analytics.

The Today screen with the outing score is free. The Hourly, Get Ready and Places tabs and every day after today in the weekly strip belong to one optional one-time unlock in the app; it is not a subscription. You can switch the app's language and temperature unit in Settings.

FAQ

Does the guide use air temperature or feels-like temperature?

Feels-like temperature, which accounts for wind and humidity. The first row of the table uses the current value; rain, UV and the day-to-night swing look at the rest of today's forecast, so the list can change during the day.

Do I need the app to use these numbers?

No. They are rules of thumb you can apply to any forecast. The app simply applies them for you every time you open it.

Is this medical advice?

No. These are suggestions only, so adjust them to how you feel and to local guidance. Babies and anyone sensitive to heat or cold may need more care than a checklist can give.
