Kid-Outing Score

A 0–100 score from feels-like temperature, UV, rain chance and wind, weighted by your child's age. 親子外出指數。

🕒

Best Time to Go Out

Finds the most comfortable window of the day for outdoor time. 最佳外出時段。

🧥

Outfit Checklist

An age- and weather-aware packing list you can tick off before leaving. 穿搭檢查清單。

🎈

Play Ideas

Over a hundred indoor and outdoor activity ideas matched to today's weather. 今天可以玩什麼。

Hour-by-hour and weekly overview, with a friendly Lumi that dresses for the weather. 逐時與一週預報。

Private by Design

Everything stays on your device. No account, no ads, no tracking. 資料只留在裝置上。

Privacy promise

Your family stays yours

Lumi Weather does not require an account and does not collect personal data. Location is used only to fetch local weather and is never uploaded to our servers, sold, or used for advertising. Weather data is provided by Apple WeatherKit.

不需要帳號、不收集個人資料。定位僅用於取得當地天氣，不會上傳、販售或用於廣告。天氣資料由 Apple WeatherKit 提供。
